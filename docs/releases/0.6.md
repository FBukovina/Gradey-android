# Gradey Android 0.6 — homework, messages, friends' timetables and the dance class

**Not published yet.** This describes the source on the `android-0.6` branch. The published release is still [0.5](0.5.md).

Version 0.6 adds the rest of what Gradey 2.8.1 has on iPhone ([iOS commit `b0677eb`](https://github.com/FBukovina/Gradely/tree/b0677eb)).

- **Homework.** Homework from Bakaláři is listed on its due day in the Planner, previewed on Today, and shown in the lesson it was given or is due in. Its sheet has the full text, teacher, dates and attachments, and a tick that tells the school it is done.
- **Messages.** An envelope on Today shows how many Komens messages are unread and opens the received messages and the school's noticeboard. Opening a message marks it read at the school. Attachments open in another app.
- **Friends' timetables.** The Timetable title's menu switches between your timetable and a friend's. A friend is added by their own Bakaláři login, which stays on the device and is used only for their timetable, or from the school's public class timetable with their groups. Friends are listed in Settings → Connected services.
- **TŠ Vavruška dance class.** An optional dance course, connected with the password from the course card: lessons, prodloužená and ball, ticket sales, the school's notice board and links on a screen of their own, the next lesson on Today, and lessons under the school day in the Timetable. An alert can be set for the moment a ticket sale opens. Turn it on in Settings → Language and preferences.
- **Absence by lesson.** Absence → By day shows which lessons each day of absence covers, and a month or quarter Recap now names the subject with the most absence.
- **Privacy notice, revision 3.** Everyone who accepted the earlier notice is shown what changed and accepts again. The summary matches the published policy of 5 November 2026.

Homework, messages and the report card need a Bakaláři account, as on iPhone. EduPage accounts do not see them.

## What is different from iPhone

- A friend's timetable cannot be read from a PDF or a photo. The two other ways of adding a friend are there.
- Ticket sale alerts are scheduled with WorkManager, so Android may deliver one a few minutes late when the phone is saving battery.
- A dance lesson's place opens in a maps app from a button on its row, where iPhone uses a long press.

## Compatibility

Version 0.6 uses internal code 9 and the existing direct-download signing key, so 0.5 and earlier direct downloads can update in place and keep their data.

Friends, their logins and the dance course are kept in encrypted storage on the device, are excluded from backups and device transfers, and are deleted on sign-out. The dance course password is sent to vavruska.info once and is not stored; only the private link to the lesson plan is.

## Verification

Verified on 20 November 2026 on the Android 17 phone emulator:

- 477 unit tests pass across the phone modules. The Wear module was not rebuilt or tested.
- All 63 phone instrumentation tests pass, in demo mode. The new ones cover homework in the Planner with its sheet and tick, messages and their read state, adding and removing a friend, connecting and disconnecting the dance class, and scheduling and cancelling ticket alerts.
- `scripts/check-design-tokens.sh` passes for 89 files. Phone lint reports 0 errors, 79 warnings and 1 hint.
- A release build (R8, baseline profile regenerated) declares version 0.6 / code 9, is 13.9 MB and has the same signing certificate as 0.5 (SHA-256 `7b02f016…286547`).
- That build installed over the published 0.5 APK. It showed the privacy update, and after accepting it opened Today with demo mode intact. Messages, adding a friend from a public timetable and connecting the dance class were then tried in it.
- The new screens were checked in dark and light mode, in English and Czech, and at 150 % text size. Two problems found at large text sizes were fixed.
- The real vavruska.info client read the site's public pages once: the notice board (7 posts), the prodloužená page (14 ticket sales) and the ticket listing (3 links).
- The real public timetable client read one school's public pages: 20 classes, and one class's weekly and permanent timetables. This found the empty Saturday row that is now left out.

Not verified:

- A real Bakaláři account. Homework, messages, marking them done or read, attachments and a friend's login were exercised with demo data and request-level tests only.
- A real dance course. Signing in with a course card password and reading a real lesson plan were exercised with sample pages only.
- A ticket alert firing at its time. Scheduling and cancelling are tested; delivery is not.
- A real phone, frame timing, and a side-by-side comparison with the iPhone app.

Known limit, shared with iPhone: the Christmas ball's page gives each sale a day but no time ("Prodej vstupenek začíná od 23. 11."), which the ticket sale reader does not match, so those sales are not listed. The prodloužená page, which gives times, is read correctly.
