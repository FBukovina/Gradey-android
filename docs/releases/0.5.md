# Gradey Android 0.5 — the iPhone app's new look, Gradey Recap and the report card

Version 0.5 rebuilds the phone app to match Gradey 2.8.1 on iPhone ([iOS commit `b0677eb`](https://github.com/FBukovina/Gradely/tree/b0677eb)).

- **A new look on every screen.** Today, Marks, Absence, Timetable, Meals, Planner, Settings, Gradey AI, onboarding and sign-in use one design system: plain headers and rows instead of cards, Space Grotesk, the same icons, colours and spacing as the iPhone app, and an Android bottom bar.
- **Gradey Recap.** Each month, quarter, semester and calendar year gets a story of its marks, average, best and toughest subject, moments, absence, timetable and school personality. A tile appears on Today when a new recap is out; Marks links to every recap so far. Any slide can be shared as an image. Everything is worked out on the device.
- **Report card.** Marks shows the mark each subject is heading for this semester, warns when an average sits within a tenth of the next mark, and lists earlier report cards. Bakaláři only.
- **Try a mark.** A subject's page shows what a new mark would do to the average as you type it.
- **Faster and smaller.** The app is now an optimized release build with a baseline profile: 13.6 MB instead of 36.1 MB. School data loads off the main thread and long lists are built lazily.

## What changed for existing users

These parts of 0.4 are gone, as they are on iPhone:

- The Gradey tools screen. Its entries moved to the top bar (Gradey AI, Settings), Timetable (Planner), Absence (Plan absence) and Settings → Support & About (Support, App updates).
- The grade history screen and the "Worth your attention" cards on Today.
- Subject search and filter chips on Marks. Subjects sort by priority or average.
- The grade simulator sheet. "Try a mark" on the subject page replaces it.

In Czech, the Absence and Meals tabs are now called **Absence** and **Jídelna**, as on iPhone.

Recap needs a semester's marks to have been loaded on this device, because Bakaláři only returns the current semester. Recaps of periods before 0.5 was installed are limited to what the school still returns.

## Availability and compatibility

[Download the phone APK](https://github.com/FBukovina/Gradey-android/releases/download/v0.5/gradey-0.5.apk) from the [0.5 release](https://github.com/FBukovina/Gradey-android/releases/tag/v0.5).

Version 0.5 uses internal code 8 and the existing direct-download signing key, so 0.4 and earlier direct downloads can update in place and keep their data; updating from 0.4 was checked. It is the first release build; earlier previews were debug builds.

Open **Settings → Support & About → App updates → Check for updates**, download the update, and confirm installation in Android. Demo installations stay in demo mode after updating.

It carries the same public Google and Supabase account configuration as 0.4. Live Gradey AI, Apple browser sign-in, Android mark-push delivery and Play purchases still need service configuration. The Wear OS companion is unchanged at 0.2.0 and is not installed by the phone updater.

## Not in this release

From iOS 2.8.1, these are not on Android yet: Komens, homework, the subjects missed on each day of absence, hidden absences, friends' timetables, TŠ Vavruška, and revision 3 of the privacy notice. Because of the third item, a month or quarter recap does not name the subject with the most absence; a semester recap does.

## Verification

Verified on 19 November 2026 on the Android 17 phone emulator:

- 420 unit tests pass across the phone modules. The Wear module was not rebuilt or tested.
- All 57 phone instrumentation tests pass, in demo mode. They cover onboarding, the five tabs, the report card, a Recap story, Planner, Plan absence, Meals ordering, Settings, the updater screen and sign-out.
- `scripts/check-design-tokens.sh` passes: no colour literals, raw text sizes, hand-typed radii or raw Material controls outside `core-ui`.
- Phone lint reports 0 errors, 70 warnings and 1 hint.
- The APK declares version 0.5 / code 8, is not debuggable, passes the 16 KiB alignment check and has the same signing certificate as 0.4 (SHA-256 `7b02f016…286547`).
- Installing it over the published 0.4 APK succeeded; the app opened straight into Today with demo mode and the privacy acceptance intact.
- After publishing, on 20 November 2026, a 0.4 installation found 0.5 under App updates, downloaded and verified it, and Android installed it.
- The main screens were checked in dark and light mode, in English and Czech, and at 150 % text size. Three problems found at large text sizes were fixed.

Not verified:

- A real phone, a real Gradey ID or a real school account. Recap and the report card were only exercised with demo data.
- Frame timing. The emulator renders in software and drops frames in every app, so it cannot show whether scrolling holds 120 fps.
- A side-by-side comparison with the iPhone app. The screens were built from the iOS source, not checked against a running iPhone build.
